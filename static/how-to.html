WORDCOMET GUIDE

How to Use WordComet as a Mentor

Set up your mentor profile, publish your availability, and conduct mock interview and guidance sessions with mentees.

STEP 01

Complete Your Mentor Profile

Open the Mentorship page and complete your mentor profile. Add the information that helps mentees understand your professional background.

  • Enter your full name
  • Select your domains or designations
  • Add your professional experience
  • Write a short professional bio
  • Upload your résumé

STEP 02

Add Your Availability

Choose the dates and times when you are available to conduct mentorship or mock interview sessions.

  • Select an available date
  • Choose your start time
  • Choose your end time
  • Create your availability

STEP 03

Wait for a Mentee to Book

Mentees can discover your profile and select one of your available time slots. Once a slot is booked, the session will appear in your bookings.

STEP 04

Review the Upcoming Session

Check your upcoming bookings before the session. Review the booking information and the résumé shared by the mentee so you can prepare for the session.

STEP 05

Join the Session

At the scheduled time, open the Session page and join the video call.

During the session you can communicate with the mentee, use your camera and microphone, chat, and conduct the mock interview or guidance session.

You're Ready to Mentor

Keep your availability updated so mentees can find a suitable time to book a session with you.

WORDCOMET GUIDE

How to Use WordComet as a Mentee

Find a mentor, choose an available time slot, book your session, and prepare for your mock interview.

STEP 01

Complete Your Profile

Make sure your WordComet profile is complete before booking a mentorship session.

STEP 02

Open Mentorship

Go to the Mentorship page to find mentors who are available for mock interviews and professional guidance.

STEP 03

Find a Mentor

Select the professional domain you are interested in and choose a mentor.

  • Select a domain
  • Select a mentor
  • Select a session date
  • Load the available time slots

STEP 04

Upload Your Résumé and Book

Upload your résumé before selecting an available time slot. Your résumé helps the mentor understand your background and prepare for the session.

Choose an available slot and click Book to confirm your mentorship session.

STEP 05

Check Your Upcoming Booking

After booking, check your upcoming sessions to confirm the date and time of your mentorship session.

STEP 06

Join Your Session

At the scheduled time, open the Session page and join your video call with the mentor.

During the session you can use your camera and microphone, chat with your mentor, and participate in the mock interview or guidance session.

You're Ready

Find a mentor and book a session whenever you are ready to practice or receive professional guidance.
